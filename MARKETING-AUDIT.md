## Executive Summary

Lorinczi Jewelry scores **72/100 (B)** — a fundamentally strong, premium, defensible brand that is currently executing at roughly 60–65% of its potential. The gap between the two numbers is almost entirely **plumbing, not strategy**: the positioning, the founder pedigree, the voice, and the SEO architecture are excellent; the proof, capture, and conversion mechanics that turn that foundation into booked commissions are missing or mis-placed.

**Biggest strength:** A genuinely rare competitive wedge — a solo artisan (metalsmithing since 12, Brown-trained, 20+ years, past work for Ralph Lauren/Nordstrom) who *accepts your own heirloom gems and gold* and works fully remote. The largest custom e-tailer, Brilliant Earth, structurally **cannot** set outside stones — meaning Lorinczi's core service is something her biggest "competitor" literally won't do. Paired with the "Metal Into Meaning" / "Let me tell your story in metal" messaging and a recently-rebuilt SEO hub-and-spoke (unique titles, schema, tight internal linking — SEO scored 81), this is a high-ceiling profile.

**Biggest gap:** **Proof and capture are absent exactly where money is decided.** The redesign service — which asks a stranger to receive your grandmother's diamond by mail — has **zero testimonials on its page**, the "Before & After" gallery (the single most conversion-driving asset in this category) contains **one** example, there is **no email capture anywhere on the site**, **no photo of Stacey**, the **Press page is empty image placeholders** (despite real coverage in Elle, Marie Claire, Brides, W, SF Magazine), and there is **no discoverable Google Business rating**. Separately, the primary high-value conversion — the free consultation — runs through a confusing **"Add to cart"** flow with no scheduler.

**Top 3 needle-movers:**
1. **Fix the consultation booking mechanic** (replace "Add to cart" → scheduler or "Request My Free Consultation" form). This is the #1 revenue path and currently the most broken step.
2. **Fill the Before & After gallery (12–20 paired examples)** and surface the existing 9 testimonials onto the redesign page. The assets largely already exist.
3. **Add email capture + a "Heirloom Redesign Guide" lead magnet** to convert the SEO traffic the site already earns into a re-marketable list for a months-long buying cycle.

**Estimated revenue impact of implementing the recommendations: ~$3,000–$7,000/month (low-to-mid five figures annually).** Because each custom/redesign commission is worth hundreds to thousands of dollars, percentage-point lifts in lead rate convert into whole commissions — the ROI here is unusually high relative to the (mostly copy-and-asset) effort required.

## Strategic Recommendations (This Month)

1. **Refocus the homepage hero.** Replace the generic "Handmade jewelry to celebrate commitments…" headline + 10 equal-weight CTAs with a benefit-led headline, a subheadline, a credibility line ("Handcrafted in San Francisco since 2002"), and a clear two-path split: *Redesign/Custom (book a free consult)* vs *Shop ready-to-ship*. Lead with the differentiated, higher-margin redesign story.
2. **Build a dedicated, scheduler-based consultation landing page** with the 5-step process, testimonials, and the safety guarantee all on one screen, so "Book a Virtual Appointment" traffic lands somewhere that closes.
3. **Create one reusable "Your jewelry is safe with me" trust module** (insured shipping, step-by-step documentation, approval-gated) and place it near the CTA on every service page. *(Also resolve the FAQ contradiction — "fully insured" vs "not responsible for lost/stolen inbound items" — with a documented Safe Mail-In Protocol.)*
4. **Launch a 4–6 email nurture sequence** for consultation no-bookers and lead-magnet downloaders, matching the multi-week consideration cycle (story → process → safety → before/after → soft consult CTA).
5. **Build a comparison page:** "Redesign with a personal designer vs. a custom e-tailer," leading with the fact that Brilliant Earth won't set outside stones — Lorinczi's exact strength — to capture comparison-stage search traffic.
6. **Add a price-anchor band** to custom + redesign (e.g., "Most redesigns range from $X–$Y") to qualify leads up and filter tire-kickers down.
