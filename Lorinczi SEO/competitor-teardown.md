## The real moat: authority
Melanie Casey wins on **domain authority** (1.5M traffic implies a large, linked-to site). That's the gap we can't shortcut — it's why **#17 (digital PR / link building)** is the highest-leverage remaining task. Our counter-moves they *don't* do:
1. **Local** — none of them target "near me" / Bay Area. Our service-area GBP is an open lane.
2. **Content cluster** — our 4 blog posts + hub-and-spoke already exceed Elora's redesign content; we can out-publish the niche.
3. **AI/GEO** — answer-first content for ChatGPT/Perplexity citations (they're not optimized for it).

## Bottom line
Beat the best page (Melanie Casey's) on quality — we're 70% there; add the named program, testimonials, lead magnet, before/after, and Article schema. Then win the lanes they ignore: **local + authority + AI citations.** The niche is winnable; authority is the work.
