# Traffic Execution Plan — lorinczi.com
_Goal: drive site traffic by leveraging existing assets. Created 2026-06-30._

## The core insight
This is NOT an awareness problem. We have **12K active Instagram followers, active Pinterest + TikTok, press history (Elle/Brides/Marie Claire/W), 6 built before/after transformations, and a distinctive catalog** — yet only ~6 site visits/day and **0 branded searches**. Attention is pooling on social and never reaching the site. The plan plugs that leak, captures the traffic, then amplifies with a small paid budget.

**Sequencing principle:** Route → Capture → Amplify → Compound. Do them in that order. Paid reach on a leaky funnel wastes money.

**Resources:** ad budget $500–2k/mo · IG/Pinterest/TikTok all very active · goal = consults + ready-to-ship + brand awareness.

## Phase 0 — Instrument (Days 1–3). Without this we're blind.
- [ ] **GA4 channel baseline:** Acquisition → Traffic acquisition. Record current sessions/day by Social / Organic / Direct / Referral.
- [ ] **UTM every social link.** One consistent scheme (`utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio` etc.) on bio, Stories, pins, TikTok. This is how we prove what works.
- [ ] **Confirm email tool** (Klaviyo/Shopify Email) is live and a capture form can be added.
- **Owner:** you supply GA4 access / numbers · **Claude builds:** UTM scheme + tracked links.
- **Success:** we know today's real channel split and can measure every change.

## Phase 1 — Plug the leak / Routing (Week 1–2). Free, highest leverage.
- [ ] **Purpose-built bio landing page** — 3 clear doors: "Start a redesign," "Shop ready-to-ship," "See before/afters." Replace homepage as the bio destination. UTM'd.
- [ ] **IG Story link stickers 2–3×/week** driving to a specific product or the before/after gallery (highest-intent click surface — currently wasted).
- [ ] **IG Shopping tags** on all catalog posts → product pages.
- [ ] **Pinterest routing audit:** every pin links to the correct page, enable **Rich Pins**, rewrite pin titles/descriptions as search queries.
- [ ] **Fix the ranking before/after blog post** — add the 6 transformation images (it currently has none).
- [ ] **Email capture on site** + lead magnet ("Heirloom Redesign Guide").
- **Claude builds:** landing page, blog image fix, pin descriptions, capture form + lead-magnet copy · **You:** post Stories, approve.
- **Success:** IG→site CTR measurable and rising; Pinterest outbound clicks up; first email signups.

## Phase 2 — Content engine / Atomize (Week 2–4, then ongoing).
- [ ] **Atomize each of the 6 transformations** into: (a) a Reel/TikTok reveal, (b) a Pinterest pin set (before / after / process), (c) its own indexable case-study page targeting a long-tail (e.g. "turn inherited charms into a necklace").
- [ ] **Repurpose top existing IG posts into Pinterest pins** (Pinterest exports clicks; IG hoards them).
- [ ] **Build a repeatable template** so every new custom job becomes 3 assets automatically.
- **Claude builds:** case-study page template + first 6 pages, pin copy, Reel scripts/shot lists · **You:** shoot/post video.
- **Success:** 6 case-study pages live + internally linked; Pinterest + Reels sending tracked traffic.

## Phase 3 — Paid amplification of the WARM audience (Week 3+). $500–2k/mo.
Do not buy cold awareness — you have it. Buy warm capture. Example split at ~$1,000/mo (scale by ROAS):
- [ ] **Retargeting (Meta) — ~50%** ($15/day): site visitors + IG engagers → consult/shop.
- [ ] **Pinterest Ads — ~30%** ($10/day): promote best-performing organic pins.
- [ ] **Google Shopping / PMax — ~20%** ($7/day): catch product intent ("rose thorn ring"). _Prereq: Google Merchant Center feed._
- **Claude builds:** audience definitions, ad copy/creative briefs, campaign structure, tracking · **You:** approve budget, connect ad accounts.
- **Success:** positive ROAS on retargeting; Pinterest/Shopping CPC and traffic tracked; scale winners.

## Phase 4 — Brand + authority compounding (Month 2–3, ongoing).
- [ ] **Press reactivation:** pitch Elle/Brides relationships + "best jewelry redesign" roundups → backlinks (lifts SEO ceiling) + referral traffic.
- [ ] **Branded-search seeding:** consistent brand name + "as seen in" across content so people start googling "Lorinczi" (currently zero).
- [ ] **UGC/influencer seeding** of transformations.
- **Success:** first backlinks land; branded-search impressions appear in Search Console; referral traffic from press.

## Operating rhythm (weekly)
- **Mon:** plan the week's Stories + pins + 1 Reel around one asset.
- **Wed:** publish; push Story link stickers.
- **Fri:** review GA4 channel numbers + ad ROAS; double down on the winner.

## Metrics
**Leading (weekly):** IG→site CTR, Story link taps, Pinterest outbound clicks, email signups, retargeting ROAS.
**Lagging (monthly):** total sessions/day, branded-search impressions (0 → growing), redesign consults, catalog sales.

## 90-day targets (realistic)
- Sessions/day: **6 → 40–80** (mostly from unlocking existing social + Pinterest, not new SEO).
- Branded search: **0 → appearing** in Search Console.
- Email list: growing from lead magnet.
- Consults + ready-to-ship sales up from the warm-traffic + retargeting loop.
