## 5. Decision gates (the go/no-go checkpoints)

Each gate = "invest in X only when metric Y crosses Z." Scored monthly (T3.7).

| Gate | Unlocks | Cross when… |
|---|---|---|
| **G1** Instagram | Meta retargeting of IG engagers | IG-attributed sessions **≥20/day for 4 straight weeks** AND ≥2 IG leads/mo |
| **G2** Email | Newsletter volume + list-growth spend | All 3 flows live AND flow CTR **≥4%** over 60 days |
| **G3** Quiz | Promoting the quiz in paid | Start→lead **≥30%** on ≥100 starts |
| **G4** Pinterest | Pinterest Ads | Organic outbound clicks **≥300/mo** AND ≥1 Pinterest lead (judge at **mo 6** on trend; kill/continue at **mo 12**) |
| **G5 / MASTER** | **All paid spend** | Site visit→lead **≥2.4%** over 60 days **AND** 3 email flows live (G2) **AND** one organic gate (G1 or G4) crossed |

Benchmarks to steer by (verified — full table + sources in GROWTH-PLAYBOOK.md): visit→lead **2.4–6.1%**, quiz start→lead **37–40%**, email flow CTR **5.58%** vs campaign 1.69%, paid search **~5.4%**, Pinterest traction **3–6 mo**.

**Do not use** (failed verification): IG DM 40–60% CTR, service-site 2–4%/7–12%, jewelry 0.95–1.46%, Pinterest reach stats. Measure DM + quiz rates first-party instead.

## 6. Operating cadence

- **Weekly (Fri):** leading indicators — IG→site CTR, Story taps, Pinterest outbound clicks, quiz starts, email signups. One double-down for next week.
- **Monthly:** gate review (T3.7) — score every gate; 1 double-down + 1 cut, defended by the benchmark table.
- **Quarterly:** re-baseline against fresh Ruler/Klaviyo/Rival IQ reports; re-check the refuted list before adopting any new number.
