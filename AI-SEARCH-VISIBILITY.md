# AI search visibility — lorinczi.com

**Date:** 7 August 2026
**Question:** how is the site doing in AI search (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity)?
**Method:** DataForSEO LLM-mentions dataset (US/en) for tracked citations, plus live queries run
through ChatGPT (web search forced) and Perplexity Sonar. Technical checks against the live site.

---

## The headline

**Better than the backlink profile deserves — and it is almost entirely one cluster.**

The site earns **12 Google AI Overview citations across 1,940 monthly AI search volume**. Every
single one is a *cost* query. Nothing else on the site is being cited by Google's AI at all.

ChatGPT knows the brand well and recommends it for local heirloom work. Perplexity does not know
it exists.

**Two things follow from this table.**

First, **the cost page is the single most valuable asset on the site for AI**, and it is the same

Second, **nothing else is cited.** No heirloom query, no ring-to-necklace query, no local query,
no life-event query appears anywhere in Google's AI citations. The redesign pages that produce 38
of 106 organic clicks produce zero AI citations.

### The citations point at the old URL — and that is fine

Every one of the 12 cites `https://lorinczi.com/blogs/journey-1/jewelry-redesign-cost`, the blog
URL that was retired in July. I checked the whole legacy cost family:

| URL | Result |
|---|---|
| `/blogs/journey-1/jewelry-redesign-cost` | 301, single hop → `/pages/jewelry-redesign-cost` |
| `/pages/how-much-does-jewelry-redesign-cost` | 301, single hop → same |
| `/pages/copy-of-how-much-does-jewelry-redesign-cost-in-san-francisco` | 301, single hop → same |

| `/pages/jewelry-redesign-cost-in-san-francisco` | 200, self-canonical (separate page, correct) |

No redirect chains, no loops, correct canonicals. A user who clicks the AI citation lands on the
right page in one hop. **Do not touch these redirects** — they are carrying the citations.
Google will re-attribute to the destination over time; that is normal and needs no action.

## 2. ChatGPT — the brand is understood, the cost content is not winning

### The brand entity is in excellent shape

Asked directly who Lorinczi Jewelry is, ChatGPT returned a genuinely accurate profile: founded by
**Stacey Lorinczi**, operating in San Francisco **since 2002**, appointment-only, Haight
neighbourhood, 94117, the Stanyan Street address, the Rose Thorn collection, heirloom redesign
using clients' own stones and gold, recycled metals and lab-grown stones.

It sourced all of that from three of the site's own pages — `about-me`,
`jewelry-redesign-san-francisco`, and the refund policy. It also resolves the business to a real
**Yelp listing**. Nothing to fix here; this is what a well-formed entity looks like.

### On local heirloom queries it recommends the studio — but in the second tier

For *"who does heirloom jewelry redesign in San Francisco"*, ChatGPT listed **seven local shops
first** (Carats & Stones, Jewelry Exchange Co, La Bijouterie, Torgsyn, Redstart Design, Ben
Shemano, French Jewelry), then a second group of "independent designers/studios that specifically
advertise heirloom transformation" — where Lorinczi appears first, described accurately as
focusing on "heirloom redesign, gemstone resetting, and turning unworn jewelry into new pieces."

The seven above the fold are all rendered as **local-business cards backed by Yelp and Google
Business Profile data** — logos, addresses, appointment links. Lorinczi is cited from its
*website*, not from a business record.

**That is the whole gap in one observation.** The top tier of a local AI answer is assembled from
local-business data, not web pages. `TRAFFIC-TREND-2026-08.md` already flags the missing Google
Business Profile as the biggest local lever; this is independent confirmation from a second
surface. It is not only the Map Pack that GBP unlocks — it is placement in AI local answers.

### On cost queries ChatGPT does not cite the site at all

Same question Google cites the site for — *"how much does it cost to reset a diamond ring"* —
ChatGPT cited only **bengarelick.com** and **brides.com**.

Worth looking at what beat it. Ben Garelick's page is a blog post titled *"How to Reset a Diamond
Ring: Options, Costs & Expert Tips (2026)"* with a **publication date of 14 July 2026** and price
bands stated as extractable ranges ($75–$250 labour, $300–$2,000 setting, $1,000–$5,000+ custom).
Freshness signal, explicit numbers, question-shaped title.

The cost page has *better* material than that — Stacey's real budget transparency, "I work
backward from your budget," "I don't take any percentage of the refining payout." It is losing on
format, not substance.

### Competitive picture

For "jewelry redesign" on ChatGPT, the most-cited domains are **katerosefinejewelry.com** (11
mentions), grantljewelry.com (9), townandcountrymag.com (7), reddit.com (7), milinalondon.com (7).
Lorinczi does not appear. On the Google AI side the site sits level with quora.com and
katerosefinejewelry.com at 12 mentions — so **Google rates it a peer of the category leader while
ChatGPT does not see it.**

## 3. Perplexity — the life-event category is completely unclaimed

Asked *"What should I do with my wedding rings after my spouse dies? Who can redesign them into
something I can wear?"*, Perplexity returned 20 sources: funeral homes, grief blogs, r/widowers,
LoveToKnow, Eterneva, mywidowedheart.com.

It said the person to do this is "a jeweler, especially one who does custom jewelry redesign,
repurposing, or heirloom redesign" — and **named no jeweler at all.**

That is the most interesting single result in this analysis. The question is being asked, the
answer explicitly points at this exact service, and **nobody owns it.** Not Lorinczi, not a
competitor. The widow and divorce pages went live on 28 July, ten days ago, which is far too early
to expect citation.

Note what Perplexity *does* cite: grief and funeral resources, not jewelers' websites. Getting
cited here is unlikely to come from on-site content alone — it will come from being mentioned on
the kind of site Perplexity already trusts for this question. The referral one-pagers already
drafted in `content-drafts/` (divorce attorneys, estate attorneys) are the right instinct aimed at
the wrong channel; the grief/funeral-resource ecosystem is where the citations live.

## 4. Technical AI readiness

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| `robots.txt` — AI crawlers | ✅ `User-agent: * / Allow: /`, no GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot blocks |
| `llms.txt` | ⚠️ present (200) but Shopify commerce boilerplate |
| `agents.md` | ⚠️ same content |
| Agentic discovery sitemap | ✅ present |
| Cost-page redirects | ✅ single-hop 301s, correct canonicals |

**On `llms.txt` and `agents.md`:** both are Shopify's auto-generated agent-commerce files. They
describe the UCP/MCP checkout endpoints, the Shop skill, and buyer-approval rules for automated
purchasing. They say **nothing about jewelry redesign, Stacey, heirloom work, or the free
consultation** — the things the business actually sells.

For a store selling ready-to-ship product that is fine. For a consultative studio where the money
is in commissions, the one file explicitly addressed to AI agents is silent about the service. I
have not tried to change it — it is generated by Shopify and may not be editable — but it is worth
checking whether it can be extended.

## 5. What to do

**1. Format the cost page for extraction — highest leverage.**
Google already treats it as a top source; ChatGPT ignores it. The gap versus Ben Garelick is
format, not quality: state the price bands as explicit ranges near the top, keep the
question-shaped H2s (already there and good), and add a visible **"last updated"** date. Freshness
is doing real work in that ChatGPT answer.

**2. Finish the Google Business Profile.**
Third independent piece of evidence now: it blocks the Map Pack, it blocks near-me queries that
already convert at 20–100%, and it keeps the studio out of the first tier of ChatGPT's local
answers, which is assembled from business records rather than web pages.

**3. Leave the cost redirects alone.**
All 12 AI citations depend on that 301. It is clean and single-hop.

**4. Claim the life-event category — the clearest open space.**
Perplexity names no jeweler for the widow question. Winning it means placement on grief and
funeral resources, not more on-site copy. Re-test on 1 September, once the widow and divorce pages
have had a full month.

**5. Extend the AI-facing files if Shopify allows it.**
Check whether `llms.txt` can carry a description of the redesign service and the consultation.

**6. Re-measure on 1 September.**
Track: AI Overview citation count (today **12**), AI search volume (today **1,940**), and whether
any non-cost page earns its first citation.

## What was checked and did not hold up

An earlier read of the raw ChatGPT entity data appeared to show the name "Lorinczi Jewelry" bound
to a competitor's Yelp record. Re-tested with a direct brand query: the entity resolves correctly
to `lorinczi.com` and the studio's own Yelp listing. The apparent mismatch was an artifact of how
the API flattens entity cards, not a real misattribution. **No action needed.**
