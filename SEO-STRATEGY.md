## 5. LLM / Answer-Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO)

This is where the user's "how much will it cost to remake my wedding band" / "will you use the gold from my ring" examples live. **The correlation:** the same BOFU question keywords that win Google also win LLM citations — *if* answered in the right format with the right schema. LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) extract **direct, structured, attributed answers** and favor brands that also appear in trusted third-party sources.

### 5a. On-page format (highest leverage)
1. **Question-as-H2 + concise direct answer** — lead each topic with the literal question as an H2, answer in the first ~40–60 words (with a real number for cost questions), then elaborate.
2. **FAQPage schema** on every service page (the `/jewelry-redesign` page already has 6 Q&As written but unmarked — fastest win).
3. **Service + LocalBusiness schema** (service = "Jewelry Redesign", areaServed = SF + Bay Area, provider = LocalBusiness with sameAs) — connects *what* to *where* as machine-readable entities.
4. **Cost transparency** — quotable price ranges; competitors stay vague, so honest numbers get cited.
5. **Author/E-E-A-T** — Stacey bio with credentials + "San Francisco, since 2002", consistent NAP, sameAs links, expanded Press page.

### 5b. The 20 BOFU questions to answer on-page (map to pillars)
**Cost:** (1) How much to remake my wedding band? (2) reset a diamond into a new setting? (3) Does using my own gold make it cheaper? (4) redesign an engagement ring? (5) turn a ring into a necklace?
**Use my materials:** (6) Will a jeweler use the gold from my old ring? (7) Can I use my own diamonds/gemstones? (8) Can you melt down my old gold? (9) Is it safe to send heirloom stones to reset?
**Process:** (10) Can I reset my diamond into a new setting? (11) Turn old rings into one new ring? (12) Old engagement ring → wedding band? (13) How long does redesign take? (14) Step-by-step process? (15) Redesign my mother's jewelry into something I'll wear?
**Local/decision:** (16) What to do with my mother's jewelry in SF? (17) Where to reset my diamond in SF/Bay Area? (18) Best heirloom-redesign jeweler in SF? (19) Redesign remotely without visiting a store? (20) Worth redesigning vs. selling?

### 5c. Off-site (what LLMs cite)
- Get into the **listicles** that rank + feed AI: SFist "Best Custom Jewelry Shops in SF", Marin Magazine "Best Jewelry Stores in Marin", Yelp "Top 10".
- **The Knot / WeddingWire** profiles + reviews (currently absent).
- Genuine **Reddit** answers (r/jewelry, r/EngagementRings) where redesign/use-your-gold comes up.
