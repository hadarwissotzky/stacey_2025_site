## 1. `/pages/use-your-own-gems-metal` — the big one

**Why this page:** it scored 2 of 19 on the exemplar audit despite being the page literally about
using your own gems and metal. It currently mentions refining in a single sentence with no
reasoning behind it. It's also the page for the "reuse your own gold / repurpose old jewelry"
cluster — roughly 790 searches/month per `SEO-STRATEGY.md`.

**Keep** the existing intro ("I can use your own gems to adapt one of my designs…") and the
closing consultation block. **Add** the following beneath the intro.

---

### WHY I REFINE YOUR METAL RATHER THAN MELTING IT STRAIGHT INTO YOUR PIECE

While I love incorporating materials that carry personal history, I generally recommend refining
old metals rather than reusing them directly for casting.

Over the years, jewelry accumulates solder, repairs, and unknown alloys that can create porosity,
pitting, or other imperfections in a new casting. The more times a metal has been melted and
recast, the greater that risk becomes. Casting also requires more metal than most people expect —
additional gold is needed to create the button and sprue that feed the casting process.

So instead, I refine the old metal and apply the settlement value toward your new piece. Refining
transforms mixed metals into a clean monetary value that becomes a credit toward the project,
while letting me cast your new piece in freshly refined metal for the best possible result.

### EVEN THE ODDS AND ENDS COUNT

Broken chains, single earrings, earring backs, outdated pieces, little scraps tucked away in a
drawer — send all of it. The more you include, the more value you receive.

### THE FULL VALUE IS YOURS

Depending on how much gold or platinum you have to refine, it's entirely possible the refining
value could exceed the cost of your new piece. In that case, you'd receive both your new jewelry
and a check for the remaining balance.

**I don't take any percentage of the refining payout.** The full value belongs to you — it can
either be applied toward the project or returned directly to you.

### HOW WE START

Send me a few clear photos of the pieces you'd like to repurpose, along with any inspiration
images or descriptions of what you're hoping to create. Reference photos of jewelry you love are
incredibly helpful — they give me a sense of your style and the direction you'd like to explore.

From there, we'll work through style, stones, metals, shape, and scale until we arrive at
something that feels exactly right. It helps to have a comfortable ballpark budget in mind — my
job is to work backward from that as much as possible, and there are often creative ways to
tailor a design so it achieves the look you want while staying in a range that feels comfortable
to you.

There's no cost to begin the conversation. Feel free to send the pics whenever you're ready —
I'm excited to see what you have.
