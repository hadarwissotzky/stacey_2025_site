# Customer Questions Research — what people actually ask (SEO + LLM)
_Source: DataForSEO (Google Ads + Labs), US, pulled 2026-07-07. Volumes = avg monthly US Google searches._
_Purpose: ground the trust-content plan in real query demand, split by whether the question lives on Google or in an AI chat._

## The headline finding — two audiences, two engines

The data splits cleanly, and the split IS the strategy:

- **Google still owns the transactional, cost, and timeline questions.** People type *"reset diamond ring cost,"* *"how long does a custom engagement ring take,"* *"turn ring into necklace"* into Google — money/logistics questions with real, measurable volume.
- **The trust/advice questions have almost no Google volume — because people now ask those to ChatGPT/Perplexity.** *"How to choose a custom jeweler," "is custom jewelry worth it," "can you reuse a diamond," "questions to ask a jeweler"* all returned **near-zero Google search volume.** That's not because nobody wonders these things — it's because they're natural-language advice questions, and that behavior has moved to AI.

**So the content plan is a barbell:**
1. **SEO pieces** target the cost / timeline / convert / before-after / "redesign" queries below — real Google volume, capture the shopper mid-decision.
2. **GEO + on-site trust pieces** answer the advice questions — written to be *cited by AI* and to *convert the on-site reader*, not to rank for volume that isn't there.
3. **The overlap pieces (cost, timeline, reuse-the-stone) are the jackpot** — high Google volume AND they're the scariest unknowns AND AI loves to cite them. Start there.

> Reality check on scale: custom/bespoke is inherently **low-volume, high-intent.** These aren't 10k-search keywords — the biggest is ~720/mo. That's fine and on-strategy: the goal is capturing the *right* person at the decision moment (and converting them), not chasing traffic. Consistent with the conversion-path-first plan.

## PART B — LLM / AI-chat questions (≈0 Google volume → asked to ChatGPT/Perplexity)

These are the **trust questions you named.** They barely register on Google — which is exactly why they're a GEO play: write them to be the clearest answer on the web so AI cites you, and to convert the human who's reading, even though they'll never drive big organic traffic. Grouped by the fear they answer.

**Methodology note:** LLM query volume isn't publicly measurable. I'm inferring "these are AI questions" from the fact that they're natural-language advice questions with near-zero Google volume — a strong inference, not a measured number.

## PART C — how this maps to content pieces (priority order)

Each piece = one page, written in first-person voice, doubling as a DM reply and atomized into an IG carousel. Ordered by leverage (overlap pieces first).

1. **"What it really costs to redesign a ring or reset a stone — and what drives the price"** → captures the entire COST cluster (590+480+110+90…), kills the scariest unknown, wildly AI-citable. **Highest leverage. Build first.**
2. **"How long does a custom piece take? My honest timeline, step by step"** → the 210+ TIMELINE cluster + de-mystifies process.
3. **"Turning a ring into a necklace or pendant — what's possible with your piece"** → the CONVERT cluster (170+90+70), your exact example, real volume.
4. **"Can I save the stone? How I assess a diamond for reuse"** → the stone-salvage LLM questions + reassures the #1 fear.
5. **"How to choose a custom jeweler (and how NOT to work with one)"** → pure trust/GEO; the heaviest trust-lifter even with no Google volume.
6. **"Redesigning a ring after a divorce / from an heirloom"** → the emotional-trigger queries (rising), high intent, deeply on-brand.
7. **Before/after case-study pages** → the BEFORE/AFTER queries + they're the proof the others link to.

Refuted/aside: local "near me" queries (480!) have volume but you're US-nationwide via virtual consults — don't build location pages; instead let the cost/timeline/convert pages capture that intent nationally.
