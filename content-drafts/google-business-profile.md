## Services (add under the Jewelry designer category; each description ≤300 chars)

1. **Jewelry redesign** — I transform jewelry you no longer wear into pieces you'll treasure — using your own gems and gold. Free consultation, transparent quote before any work begins.
2. **Diamond & gemstone resetting** — I reset your diamond or gemstone into a new setting designed around you — from a fresh take on a classic to something entirely new.
3. **Heirloom & inherited jewelry redesign** — Inherited jewelry that isn't your style? I redesign your mother's or grandmother's pieces into jewelry that honors the story and fits your life.
4. **Turn a ring into a necklace** — One of my most popular redesigns: your ring becomes a pendant or necklace, keeping the stones and sentiment in a form you'll reach for daily.
5. **Custom engagement rings & wedding bands** — One-of-a-kind engagement rings and hand-carved wedding bands, made just for you in silver, gold, platinum, or palladium.
6. **Use your own gold & stones** — I responsibly refine your old gold and reuse your stones in the new design — lowering cost and keeping the original metal in the piece.
7. **Virtual consultations** — Free online consultation. Send photos of your pieces, and I'll walk you through options and give you a transparent quote — everything can be done remotely.
