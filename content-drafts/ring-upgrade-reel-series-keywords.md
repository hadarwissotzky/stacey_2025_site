# "I Love My Husband — Just Not the Ring"
## Keyword & hashtag plan for the ring transformation reel series

**Prepared:** August 2026
**Series:** 4–5 reels following one client's engagement ring transformation — a ring whose
style she outgrew, redesigned into something that suits who she is now.
**Search data:** Google / DataForSEO, United States, 12-month trailing.

---

## The headline finding

**"Engagement ring upgrade" gets 720 searches a month** — 3.5× "engagement ring redesign"
(210) and 14× "redesign engagement ring after divorce" (50).

The trend lines matter even more than the volume:

- Divorce-framed redesign searches are **down 43% year over year**
- "Redesign old engagement ring" is **up 100%**
- "Engagement ring redesign ideas" is **up 100%**

The non-divorce framing isn't just a bigger audience — it's the growing one.

**One caveat on "upgrade":** part of that 720 is women wanting a *bigger diamond*. It's a
$3.22 CPC term and jewelers bid hard on it for trade-in programs. This series is about style,
not carat size. Use the word — that's where the searches are — and let the hook do the
reframing.

## Stacking keywords — one per reel

Don't repeat a single phrase five times. Stack a different one per reel so the series covers
the whole search space and the reels don't compete with each other.

For each reel, place the keyword in **three** spots: spoken aloud in the first 3 seconds,
on-screen as text, and in the first line of the caption.

| # | Beat | Primary keyword | Secondary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The hook | `I don't like my engagement ring` | `love the man, not the ring` |
| 2 | The why | `outgrew my engagement ring` | `engagement ring I chose at 25` |
| 3 | The process | `reset engagement ring` | `keep your own diamond` |
| 4 | The reveal | `engagement ring redesign before and after` | `engagement ring transformation` |
| 5 | The objection | `engagement ring upgrade` | `redesign engagement ring cost` |

### Title lines worth testing

- "I love my husband. I just don't love the ring."
- "The ring didn't change. I did."

## Hashtags

### Instagram — 5

```
#engagementringredesign  #ringredesign  #customengagementring  #jewelryredesign  #ringsofinstagram
```

Four niche tags plus one reach tag. Instagram now leans on caption keywords far more than
hashtags, so these are there to place the post in a category — not to win reach on their own.

### TikTok — 5

```
#jewelrytok  #ringtransformation  #engagementringupgrade  #customjeweler  #beforeandafter
```

`#jewelrytok` and `#beforeandafter` are the discovery surface. The middle three tell the
algorithm what the video actually is.

**Skip `#fyp`.** It does nothing but consume a slot.

---

## What matters more than hashtags

Both platforms transcribe audio and read on-screen text, and both are now used as search
engines in their own right.

**Saying "I don't like my engagement ring" out loud in reel 1 will do more than all five
hashtags combined.**

The three-placement rule again, because it's the whole game:

1. **Spoken** in the first 3 seconds
2. **On-screen** as text
3. **First line** of the caption

## Before you post

- **Check hashtag sizes in-app.** The search volumes in this doc are Google data and solid.
  The hashtag picks are reasoned from search demand and platform mechanics, not measured
  counts — reliable live hashtag sizing isn't publicly available and goes stale fast.
- **On Instagram, aim for tags in the 10k–500k range.** Anything over a few million buries you.
- **Reel 4 is the one to boost.** "Before and after" carries both the highest save rate and a
  real search term behind it.
