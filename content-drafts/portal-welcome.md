# Bloom client portal — welcome greeting

On-screen copy, not an email. Sits under "Welcome to your client portal!"

Third in the set with `contact-confirmation-email.md` and
`appointment-confirmation-email.md`.

---

## Primary

**Welcome to your client portal!**

This is where everything for your project lives — the photos you send me, the
designs we shape together, and your estimates all in one place.

As your piece is made, I'll post pictures at every stage, so you're never in the
dark about how it's coming along.

Anything at all you'd like to ask, just message me here. It's a one-person studio,
so it's always me at the other end.

— Stacey

## Note on the middle line

"Pictures at every stage, so you're never in the dark" is not new phrasing — it's
Stacey's own answer to the thing she says worries new clients most. From the David
email:

> "The two most pressing concerns new clients tend to have are the opacity of the
> fabrication process (will it come out just as I imagine?), and the cost. The
> first I tackle by providing pictures of the ring throughout all stages of its
> production, so you're never in the dark about what it looks like."

The portal is the answer to that worry, so the greeting is the right place to make
the promise rather than leaving clients to discover it.

"It's always me at the other end" carries the solo-studio point without repeating
"there's no team here, it's just me" from the contact email — the same idea, so
worth varying for anyone who reads both.
