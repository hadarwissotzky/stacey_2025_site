Whether it's revitalizing a cherished heirloom, adapting one of my designs, or creating something new, I’m happy to use your gems. Using your materials preserves sentimental value, cuts costs, and reduces environmental impact.

I’m committed to transparent, eco-friendly metal refining and straightforward pricing, working within your budget and with the materials you provide.
