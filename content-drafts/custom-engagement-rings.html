How We Design Your Ring

  1. Consultation. We start with a conversation — virtual or in person — about what you picture: the stone, the metal, the feeling, the budget. No idea is too vague; that's my job to shape.
  2. Sketch & design. I turn our conversation into sketches and detailed designs so you can see your ring before it exists. We refine it together until it's exactly right.
  3. Approve & handcraft. Once you love the design, I handcraft the ring myself, keeping you updated along the way.
  4. Your ring. You receive a one-of-a-kind engagement ring no one else in the world will ever wear.
