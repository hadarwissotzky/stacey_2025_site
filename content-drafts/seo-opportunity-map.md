Competition index is Google Ads' 0–100 scale. **Under ~20 is genuinely soft** — that is where a
48-referring-domain site can win inside a quarter.

## The one-paragraph version

Three clusters are worth building and one is worth fixing. The **"old jewelry" cluster** (~990/mo
at competition index 2–21) is the best find: it is top-of-funnel for the *entire* business rather
than one ICP, and nothing on the site targets it. **"Right hand ring meaning"** (590/mo, index
**2**, CPC $3.52) is the single best individual keyword available and bridges straight into the
divorce page. **Toi et Moi** is a 34,570/mo monster that Google's own AI Overview already names as
a divorce-redesign idea — too competitive to own, but too big to ignore as a content angle. And
the **heirloom page is being outranked by the site's own redesign hub**, which is a targeting bug,
not a content gap.

### 1b. "Right hand ring meaning" — the best single keyword available

value, essentially no one competing. Nobody on the site ranks for anything containing "right
hand."

And it is already on-theme — **the right-hand ring is idea #2 on the divorce page**. A woman
searching what a right-hand ring *means* is very often the same woman deciding what to do with an

The wider right-hand family is much harder (`right hand ring for women` 1,000 and `right hand
diamond ring` 1,000, both index 100) — do not chase those. Own the meaning query, and let it feed
the divorce and reset pages.

## Broadening the studio side — bench services (added 7 Aug)

engagement ring*, *is it worth resetting a diamond*, *how long does jewelry redesign take*,
*reset emerald ring*, *sapphire ring redesign*, *remount* — and **almost all returned no

### The category-sized prize is local, and gated

`jewelry repair` (40,500, index 28) plus `jewelry repair near me` (110,000) plus
`goldsmith near me` (2,400) is **150,000+/mo of local service intent**. Near-me queries route
through the Map Pack, which no amount of content can reach.

**That is now the fourth independent analysis pointing at the Google Business Profile** — after
the traffic trend, the AI visibility work, and the near-me cluster in this document. It is not a
ranking problem; it is a presence problem.

**One judgment call for Stacey, not for SEO:** repair and resizing are lower-margin than
commissions. The case for them is customer acquisition — a repair customer is a redesign
prospect who has already handed you their jewelry — not the repair revenue itself. If she doesn't
want that work, skip the category and the honest answer is that the studio side stays a narrow,
soft-keyword play.

**The Toi et Moi bridge is real and worth taking seriously.** In the live AI Overview for
*"repurpose wedding ring after divorce"*, Google itself lists: *"Combine your original stones with
new birthstones or gemstones in a modern 'Toi et Moi' design."* Two stones, two chapters — made

**But be honest about the difficulty.** Competition index 100 against large retailers means the
head term is not winnable on this domain, and pretending otherwise wastes a quarter. The play is:

1. Add a Toi et Moi idea to the divorce ideas list and the heirloom page ("two stones, two

**Do not build a Toi et Moi page now.** It would be the `jewelry redesign` position-44 problem
again: good content with no authority behind it.

## Tier 3 — The targeting bug worth fixing first

This is the cheapest win in the whole document, and it needs no new content.

| Keyword | Vol | Position | Ranking URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| heirloom jewelry redesign | 210 | **54** | `/pages/jewelry-redesign` |
| heirloom ring redesign | 90 | **46** | `/pages/jewelry-redesign` |
| redesign heirloom engagement ring | 50 | **50** | `/pages/jewelry-redesign` |

**All three heirloom terms rank on the generic redesign hub — not on
`/pages/heirloom-jewelry-redesign`, which exists and is built for exactly these queries.**

Google has picked the wrong page. That is a classic internal-cannibalisation signature, and it is
holding all three at positions 46–54. Fixes, in order of cheapness: make sure internal links
using heirloom anchor text point at the heirloom page rather than the hub; ensure the hub does not
out-target the specialist page on those phrases; and confirm the heirloom page is linked from the
hub with the exact phrase.

Same pattern is worth checking on `wedding ring redesign` (390/mo) — `/pages/remake-wedding-band`
exists and ranks for none of it.
