QUICK RULES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  • First person. "I" to "you". Never "we/our" as the business, the only "we"
    is Stacey + the client: "we'll work through style, stones, metals and scale."

  • Name the actual materials. "Her mother's 14k" beats "gold". "28 small
    diamonds" beats "diamonds".

  • End with an invitation, not a pitch. Her own lines:
      Send me the pics whenever you're ready.
      There's no cost to begin the conversation.
      It doesn't need to be exact.
      You don't have to decide anything yet.

  • On anything inherited after a death, never "closure", "moving on", "letting
    go", "healing", and no anniversary or timing hooks.

  • Theme 2 vs Theme 3: if someone died, it's Theme 2 and the register is
    careful. If she simply outgrew it, it's Theme 3 and the register can be
    light, even funny. Don't put grief language on a woman who just changed her
    mind about a ring, and don't be breezy about a piece someone left her.

  • Bezel, explained in one line if it's needed: the metal wraps the stone
    instead of gripping it with prongs, so there's nothing to catch or bend.

  • No em-dashes. Use a comma or a full stop. This matches the site, where every
    em-dash was replaced in September 2026.
