# The US jewelry search landscape, and where lorinczi.com actually sits in it

**Date:** 20 August 2026
**Method:** four parallel research streams — competitor mining, systematic keyword discovery
(17,277 keywords), multi-year trend analysis (35 keywords, 5 years of monthly data), and an
exhaustive owner-intent map (26,633 keywords, 55 discovery sweeps).
**Sources:** DataForSEO Labs, Google Ads volume (validated), Google Trends, live SERPs, Google
Search Console.

---

## The short version

I had been mapping the wrong axis.

The **phrase patterns** I'd been enumerating — *"what to do with X"*, *"turn X into Y"*,
*"redesign"* — really are close to exhausted. That part of my earlier "the space is finite" call
holds up, and now it's provable rather than asserted. But those are three patterns out of a much
larger space, and they are the *low-value* three.

Owners overwhelmingly express themselves with **repair / reset / appraise** verbs, applied to
**non-ring objects**. That territory is almost entirely unclaimed, carries CPCs of $4–$10 against
$2–$5 for the "what to do with" family, and lorinczi.com has zero presence in it.

**The single biggest structural finding: this is a ring-shaped site in a market where chains,
flatware, and brooches are what's actually sitting in the drawer.**

So the instinct was right, and it's now settled: **don't keep looking for more "turn X into Y"
pages.** There are 21 of them in existence and we've built against the best one.

### Four patterns return literally zero at ≥20/mo

This is the uncomfortable part.

- *"[object] I never wear"* — zero
- *"sitting in a drawer"* — zero
- *"declutter jewelry"* — zero
- **every widow / spouse-death phrasing** — widow, passed away, deceased, late husband — **zero**
- *"bring your own diamond" / "use my own stone"* — zero

**People feel those things. They do not type them.** That doesn't make the widow page wrong — it
makes it *copy*, not a *keyword target*. Keep the page, keep the emotional register in
ICP-COMMUNICATION-PLAYBOOK.md, keep it in email. Stop expecting it to bring traffic, and don't
build more pages on that axis.

The grief cluster that *does* have volume is a different one: **ashes into jewelry**
(`turn ashes into jewelry` 1,000/mo, plus pet variants).

## Part 4 — What the market is actually doing (trends)

**Baseline first: median YoY across 35 jewelry keywords is −6.2%.** The whole category is drifting
down as AI Overviews absorb informational clicks. Flat is a win. Read every number below against
that baseline, not against zero.

### The "modern heirloom" divergence

Two clusters that sound identical are moving in opposite directions.

**Buying an actual old ring is falling:**
vintage engagement ring −16% / −45% (2yr) · antique engagement ring −7% / −26% · estate jewelry
−9% / −20%

**Commissioning new work with antique character is roughly doubling:**

| | YoY | 2-year |
|---|---|---|
| colored gemstone engagement ring | **+107%** | +137% |
| vintage inspired engagement ring | **+102%** | +141% |
| old mine cut diamond | **+91%** | +143% |
| hand engraved ring | +26% | +100% |
| bezel set engagement ring | +26% | +70% |

The old-mine-cut shift is visible in the raw series, not inferred: 4,400–6,600/mo through mid-2025,
then 9,900–18,100 every month since September.

**This isn't an adjacent opportunity — it's Stacey's existing business arriving as a market
trend.** The catch is that the head terms are competition index 100 and belong to Brilliant Earth,
Blue Nile and Etsy. A solo studio doesn't win "vintage inspired engagement ring." It can win the
intersection nobody else can supply: *antique character, made from the stone you already own.*

### The calendar runs backwards from the industry's

| Cluster | Peaks |
|---|---|
| jewelry redesign | **May–July** (index 1.30) |
| heirloom jewelry | **July–August** (1.64) |
| custom engagement ring | **August** (1.74 — lead time before December proposals) |
| engagement ring *(gift intent)* | December (1.20) |
| mothers ring *(gift intent)* | December (2.00) |

**Her core service peaks when the industry's marketing calendar is asleep.** Pitch redesign
May–August, commissions in August, and treat Nov–Dec as gift season rather than commission season.

### Gold: lead with money, not virtue

`sell gold near me` is 40,500/mo, **+26% YoY / +54% 2yr** — people are cashing in drawer jewelry at
record prices. But sustainability language is *not* growing: recycled gold **−26%**, upcycled
flat-to-down. That contradicts the sustainability framing in the site's existing copy. The winning
frame is "you already own the gold, so you're not buying it twice."

## Part 6 — What this round overturned

Four claims I made earlier in this project did not survive the research:

1. **"Permanent jewelry is up 82%."** That was its seasonal index (0.79 Jan → 1.43 Jul), not
   growth. Like-for-like it is **−23% YoY**, peaked mid-2024. We did not build for it; we came
   close.
2. **"Plan a January divorce-ring campaign."** The Dec→Jan ratio was 1.00, 0.79, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00
   across 2021–2025 — and 2.25× in 2026 only. There is no stable January peak. What's actually
   true is better: **every month of 2026 runs ~2× its 2025 counterpart.** It's a small,
   genuinely growing, *aseasonal* category.
3. **"The owner-intent space is finite and nearly mapped."** Half right. The *phrase patterns* are
   finite and mapped. The *verb axis* and the *non-ring objects* were untouched, and they're worth
   more than what we mapped.
4. **"Redesign demand is a gifting-season business."** It peaks May–August. The industry's
   calendar is not this business's calendar.

Plus one from earlier that's now confirmed rather than suspected: **backlinks were never the
binding constraint.** Clicks went 39 → 105 with zero link building.

## Part 7 — What I could not verify

- **Local volume.** Everything above is national US. Bay Area slices will be roughly 1–3% of these
  figures. I did not pull SF-scoped data.
- **`jewelry repair near me` at 110,000/mo** came from Labs synonym clustering and was not
  re-validated through Google Ads. Directionally enormous, not literal.
- **Cluster totals sum overlapping synonym variants and overstate true demand.** The bolded
  per-keyword numbers are the trustworthy ones; cluster sums are for relative ranking only.
- **Non-US English.** "Remodelling" and "valuation" skew UK/AU; I swept US only.
- **`old mine cut diamond` reports 22,200/mo but August 2025 was 110,000** — Taylor Swift's
  engagement ring. Real baseline is ~12,000, still up 91% YoY on a like-for-like basis.
- **Whether the zero-volume emotional phrasings convert well when someone lands on them another
  way.** Absence of search volume proves nobody types it. It does not prove the emotion is absent.
